FamJam Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

FamJam is a private family organizer used by the Hill family. It is run personally by Brian Hill and is not offered to the public. This policy explains what FamJam collects and how it uses it.

What FamJam collects

  • Account: your email address and sign-in data.
  • Household content: family member names, calendar events, chores, lists, meal plans and other items you add.
  • Google Calendar (optional): if you connect Google, FamJam reads your calendar events and your Google account email address. Access is read-only. FamJam never creates, changes or deletes anything in your Google Calendar.

How Google data is used

FamJam uses Google Calendar data only to show your events inside your own family's FamJam household. Events are stored with your household's records. The Google sign-in token is stored encrypted on FamJam's server and is used only to refresh your events.

If you turn on FamJam's optional voice assistant, the titles and times of events you ask about may be sent to OpenAI to answer that request. FamJam does not use Google data to train AI models, does not sell it, and does not use it for advertising.

FamJam's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who processes data

FamJam stores data with Supabase (database and server functions). Push notifications go through Expo and Apple or Google. Optional AI features use OpenAI. These services process data only to run those features.

Removing data

Disconnecting Google in FamJam's calendar settings removes the stored Google token and the events imported from that calendar. You can also revoke FamJam's access at any time from your Google Account's security settings. To have your FamJam account or household data removed, email the address below.

Children

Children's profiles are created and managed by a parent. Children do not connect Google accounts.
